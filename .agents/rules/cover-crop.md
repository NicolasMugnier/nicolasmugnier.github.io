# Cover crop

The same WebP is used in three `object-fit: cover` frames, all centered:

1. Article `.post-cover img`: full content width, `max-height: 400px`. Wide banner. A 1280x720 file keeps about the **middle 40 to 50% of the height**.
2. Home cards: `aspect-ratio: 16 / 10`.
3. Home hero: 50% width, `min-height: 300px`.

Rules:

- Canvas 1280x720.
- Motif in a center band: about 70% width by 40% height, vertically centered (y roughly 30% to 70%).
- One motif. No two-row diagrams, corner legends, or small edge labels.
- Atmosphere (grid, vignette) may fill the cropped bands. The strip that remains must still be readable.
- Detailed diagrams go in the article body (Mermaid), not on the cover.
